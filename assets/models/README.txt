============================================================
RIG STUDIO - 3D HARDWARE MODEL DROP ZONE
============================================================

Drop your .glb 3D models directly into this folder!
The WebGL scene automatically detects and loads them:

1. Graphics Card:
   Filename: gpu.glb
   Position: Vertical PCIe showcase slot facing side glass

2. CPU Cooler:
   Filename: cooler.glb
   Position: Mounted directly over the CPU socket

3. Motherboard:
   Filename: mobo.glb
   Position: Mounted on the chassis motherboard tray

4. RAM:
   Filename: ram.glb
   Position: Mounted into the DDR5 DIMM slots

5. Power Supply:
   Filename: psu.glb
   Position: Mounted in rear lower PSU chamber

6. Chassis:
   Filename: case.glb
   Position: Outer chassis frame

Tip: .glb (Binary glTF) is recommended as it bundles geometry,
materials, and textures into a single high-performance file.
============================================================
